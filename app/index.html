Oracle and exit keys

The pasted addresses are where an off-chain payout goes. A seller wallet mnemonic is the 12 or 24 words from Arkade.Money. On mutinynet those words use the testnet BIP86 key. An nsec or 64-character hex key works too. Either field can be left blank.
